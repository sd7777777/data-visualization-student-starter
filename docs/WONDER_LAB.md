# Wonder lab

Three interactive SVG experiments, added locally in September 2026. Open the **Wonder lab** chapter after Relationships, or use `/#wonder-lab`.

- **Share currents**: connected stacked shares of provider records, claims, and cost. Switch geography, choose a specialty, change ordering, and inspect ribbons or legend buttons. The largest eight available specialties plus the selection get separate ribbons. The residual is calculated from the complete geography totals, so omitted specialties are included. These are comparisons of aggregate shares, not observed flows.
- **State orbit**: radial lollipops on a symmetric logarithmic ratio scale around each specialty's national benchmark. Choose among 18 profiles and two measures, filter by provider count, reorder states, or find the largest absolute log-ratio departure. State abbreviations carry no spatial meaning. Coverage is disclosed; excluded or zero values are not plotted. The scale adapts to the filtered data. The orbit has its own specialty selector initialized from the current inspection because not every geography's top 32 includes all 18 profiles.
- **Rank weave**: curved parallel rank axes for cost/claim, claims/provider, reported opioid share, and reported antibiotic share. Trace or isolate a specialty, rotate axis order, and find the greatest rank spread. Ranks use the current geography's available summary subset. Equal values share a rank; the next rank skips tied positions. Category suppression limits remain disclosed.

The share and weave selectors link to the existing geography and inspected-specialty state. All marks support focus and Enter/Space. Native selectors provide a name-based alternative. Chart viewports scroll independently on narrow screens. Transitions respect reduced motion. No new dependency or external service is used.

Implementation: `components/analysis/wonder-lab.tsx`, `lib/wonder-analysis.ts`, and `app/wonder-lab.css`.

Verification: 5,622 share-conservation/selection cases across 63 geographies; rank correctness including ties; reciprocal orbit symmetry and finite bounds; existing interaction, geography, and evidence checks; TypeScript; targeted lint; production static build. Browser review covered experiment switching, linked selection, a two-specialty geography, orbit filtering to zero states, outlier finding, rank isolation, axis rotation, and narrow-screen overflow. Safari screenshots are in the handoff's `review/wonder-lab/` directory. No end-user study has been conducted.

## Geographic experiments — September 26 continuation

The lab now opens on Hex atlas and has six modes. Original currents, orbit, and weave remain available.

- **Hex atlas:** 51 schematic state/DC cells; color is state value divided by the selected specialty's national value. Fixed bands are <0.5, [0.5,0.8), [0.8,1.2], (1.2,2], and >2 times national. Optional inner hexagon area represents provider records, scaled to the largest included state. Cell outlines remain equal. The state dropdown is equivalent to clicking or keyboard-activating a map mark. Positions are adapted from the pre-existing tile map and do not represent boundaries or distances; AK and HI are insets.
- **State garden:** four fixed-angle sectors encode midrank percentiles for cost/claim, claims/provider, reported opioid share, and reported antibiotic share. Radius is proportional to square root of percentile, so sector area is proportional to percentile. The dashed reference is 50th percentile and the outer circle is 100th. Ties share their percentile midpoint. Show twelve states ordered alphabetically or by summed absolute deviation from 50 across the four measures. The selected state replaces the twelfth card when necessary, visibly marked PINNED. A state dropdown makes every eligible state accessible. Exact values and percentiles appear below.
- **Claim mountains:** state distributions for the seven specialties with highest national total cost plus the selected specialty, or all 18 profiles. Twenty shared equal-width bins form frequency polygons. The default compares multiples of each specialty's national value; an absolute-value toggle preserves original units. Every state has equal weight. A single frequency-height scale applies to every ridge, while a dashed line marks the national benchmark. Focus/click a bin to inspect its members and actual values. Bins include the rightmost endpoint and exclude missing observations; they are not smoothed densities. Missing/zero national denominators exclude the row from relative calculations. Axes rescale with filters and displayed specialties.

All three views cover the 50 states plus DC, exclude territories, and support thresholds of 1, 50, 250 or 1,000 provider records. They use the existing 2024 CMS aggregates, not synthetic data. Specialty selection links to the rest of the page. Selecting a specialty absent from the currently selected geography resets that geography to US so the selection can be represented. State selection and coverage controls stay within the atlas views. Suppressed category shares remain conservative lower bounds. Percentiles and distinctiveness scores are descriptive, not statistical significance or care-quality scores.

Validation: `node scripts/check_atlas.mjs` checks all 51 unique cells, 360 metric/threshold combinations, distribution conservation, ties, empty results, and finite petal geometry. Existing Wonder lab, interaction and geography checks remain required. ## Composition experiments — September 26 continuation

The lab now has nine modes and opens on **Spending mosaic**. Three additions use the current geography and linked specialty selection:

- **Spending mosaic:** a balanced binary treemap of all available specialties plus a striped aggregate for the remainder. Switch rectangle area among provider records, claims, and drug cost. Color stays fixed to specialty cost per claim divided by the overall geography cost per claim, using the displayed five bins. Rectangles are proportional before separator strokes; layout changes only with geography or sizing, not specialty selection. Small categories have an accessible name directory, exact readouts, and native specialty selection. Selecting the remainder does not impersonate a specialty or change the linked page selection.
- **Cost skyline:** a variable-width bar chart. Width is claim share and height is cost per claim; alternatively width is provider-record share and height is cost per record. Consequently, each tower’s share of total filled area equals its share of drug cost. Twelve largest available specialties by total cost appear separately, plus a selected specialty outside those twelve; everything else is a striped residual. Purple towers exceed the geography-wide weighted mean, teal towers are at or below it. Height is linear and rescales with the measure or included specialties. A numbered name key keeps very narrow towers accessible. These are profiles, not a geographic city map.
- **Concentration lens:** extends the earlier single-measure cumulative chart into a common-order comparison of provider records, claims, and cost. Change ordering, move the rank slider, click or keyboard-activate a stop, find the 80% point, jump to the selected specialty, or trace the last added specialty throughout the page. All three curves follow the same available-specialty ordering; totals use full-geography denominators and the coverage note explains why endpoints can remain below 100%. The omitted specialty group is not ranked as one specialty. Equal ordering values break ties alphabetically.

Dollar totals in the bundled summary are rounded independently. AE, AP and GU specialty sums exceed geography totals by $1–$2; raw readouts retain these numbers. Treemap geometry normalizes to the sum of its tile values and cumulative percentage geometry caps at 100% to handle this rounding. No source observations were altered. Positive shares below 0.1% are labeled `<0.1%`, not zero.
